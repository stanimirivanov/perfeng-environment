# Code of conduct

## TL;DR

- Treat contributors and users with dignity, patience and professional care.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and disclosure of private
  information are unacceptable.
- Report conduct concerns privately whenever possible.
- Maintainers will act proportionately and protect reporter confidentiality as
  far as practical.

## Our commitment

Performance engineering platform contributors, maintainers and community
members commit to a welcoming, safe and equitable environment. Participation
is open to people acting in good faith regardless of identity, background,
experience, ability or viewpoint.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).

## Expected and unacceptable behavior

Expected behavior includes empathy, constructive technical disagreement,
respect for boundaries, responsible handling of mistakes and attention to the
effect of words and actions on others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; threats; stalking; discriminatory,
demeaning or sexualized language or attention; sustained disruption; personal
attacks; retaliation; and publication of another person's private information
without permission. Technical criticism is welcome when it is specific,
evidence-based and directed at the work rather than the person.

## Scope, reporting and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere. Report a concern privately to a maintainer using a
private contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If no private
channel is available, request one without identifying people or describing the
incident publicly. An immediate threat should be reported to the appropriate
local authority or platform safety team.

Maintainers MUST handle reports promptly, impartially and as confidentially as
practical. They MAY remove content, issue a private correction or warning,
limit participation temporarily, or ban participation permanently according to
the behavior's impact and pattern. A maintainer involved in a report MUST
recuse themselves when another suitable reviewer is available. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
